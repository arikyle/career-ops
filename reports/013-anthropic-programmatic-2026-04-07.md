## A — Role Summary

Anthropic is hiring a Head of Programmatic Outcomes (Partners) to build and run the partner certification, enablement, and activation infrastructure across SIs, consulting firms, MSPs, and technology partners. The goal: scale Anthropic's customer activation motions through partners at 10x, without adding Anthropic headcount. This is not a traditional channel/alliances role — it sits at the intersection of Partnerships, Sales, Product, and Growth Marketing, holding the "connective tissue" that turns existing partner relationships into a reliable activation channel.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Design and drive Anthropic Certified Partner Program (tiers, certification, enablement, co-activation frameworks)
- Recruit and onboard highest-leverage partners (SIs, consulting firms, MSPs, tech partners) for Tier C account AI adoption
- Co-develop partner enablement toolkit with Product and Growth Marketing
- Design partner feedback loop and performance metrics
- Serve as internal authority on partner-led activation; resolve channel conflict across Alliances, Sales, Marketing

**Location:** San Francisco, CA or New York City, NY (hybrid, 25% in-office minimum)
**Comp:** $315,000 – $350,000 USD annual salary (no equity range published, but Anthropic is known for significant equity grants)
