## C) Level & Strategy

### Level Assessment

- **JD level:** Director of Product Management — individual-contributor-heavy ("mini-CEO of use cases"), 6-12 years experience
- **Ari's natural level for this archetype:** Director-level by experience breadth (10+ years, multi-company, founder), but the specific PM-in-AI domain is where he'd be entering more junior than peers who have 6+ years of dedicated PM work on ML/AI products

### "Sell Senior Without Lying" Plan

1. Lead with the **builder identity**: "I've been the product leader for every venture I've founded — from architecture to customer discovery to GTM. At i2o, my role expanded into product strategy, roadmap prioritization, and launching new data products."
2. Emphasize **retail ecommerce domain overlap**: "CommerceIQ operates in exactly the space I know — retail media intelligence, digital shelf analytics, brand-platform dynamics. I was at i2o building competitive products."
3. Position **AI prototyping as a strength**: "I build with code — Python, Next.js, ML models. My prediction market bot uses a full ML stack. I prototype first, validate with users, then scale."

### "If They Downlevel" Plan

- Unlikely to be downleveled further since Director is the posted level
- If they suggest Senior PM instead: accept if base is $200K+ and there's a clear 12-month path to Director with defined criteria
- Negotiate equity upside given the unicorn stage
