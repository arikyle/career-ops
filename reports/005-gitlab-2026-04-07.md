## Summary

GitLab is hiring a Director of Product Management to lead strategy for the Duo Agent Platform — their AI agent builder and ecosystem layer. The role owns interoperability across model providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google), multi-agent patterns, MCP integrations, and adoption of GitLab's out-of-the-box agents and AI catalog. Reports to VP of Product, AI. Fully remote (US, Canada, EMEA).

---

## A — Match with CV (2.5/5)

**What they want:** A product management leader with direct experience building agent builder products, defining agent instructions/tool use, and shipping developer experience for agentic AI platforms. Deep knowledge of multi-agent systems, MCP, and interoperability patterns across AI providers.

**What Ari brings:**
- Product strategy and roadmap influence at i2o Retail (expanded into growth and product strategy, roadmap prioritization, launched benchmark products).
- Full product lifecycle as serial founder: multi-tenant platform architecture (Koqi), SaaS products (SkySuite, SwingBase), ML stack (prediction market bot with LightGBM).
- Technical fluency: Next.js, React, Python, Azure. Hands-on builder.
- API and platform integration experience (Skai, Pacvue integrations at i2o; partner onboarding at Amobee).

**Gaps:**
- No direct product management title or PM org leadership. Ari's PM experience is as a founder/operator, not leading a PM team inside a scaled tech company.
- No demonstrated experience building agent builder products, defining agent instructions, or shipping developer-facing agentic AI tooling. This is the core ask.
- No public track record with multi-agent systems, MCP, or AI agent interoperability standards — which are explicit requirements.
- The role is deeply technical PM in the AI infrastructure/developer tools space. Ari's strength is commercial/partnership/GTM, not dev tools product management.

**Bottom line:** Ari has adjacent technical chops and product intuition from his founder work, but the JD specifically requires experience building agent builder products and shipping agentic AI developer experience. That is a significant gap.

These are substantive gaps, not just narrative challenges.

## Recommendation: SKIP

**The comp and culture are excellent, but the role-candidate fit is weak.** This is a deeply technical product management role in agentic AI infrastructure — not a partnerships, BD, or commercial product role. The JD explicitly requires experience building agent builder products and shipping agentic AI developer experience, which Ari does not have.

Ari's best path into GitLab would be through a partnerships/ecosystem-focused role (they have those separately) rather than a pure PM role in their AI platform org.

**If Ari wants to pursue anyway:** Lead with the founder/builder angle (architected multi-tenant platforms, specs ML systems, codes in production), emphasize the Skai/Pacvue platform integration work, and frame the prediction market bot as evidence of ML/AI systems thinking. But expect to be competing against candidates with direct agentic AI product management experience.
