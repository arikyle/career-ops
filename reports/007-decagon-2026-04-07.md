## Role Summary

The Head of Strategic Partnerships is responsible for building and scaling Decagon's partner ecosystem -- including ISVs, VARs, SIs, consulting organizations, investors, and hyperscalers (GCP, AWS, Azure). The role owns the Partner GTM Channel: marketing, positioning, and reselling AI-powered solutions through partners.

### Key Responsibilities (from JD)

- Create and navigate an agile partner network (ISVs, VARs, SIs, consulting firms, investors)
- Develop an independent, partner-driven channel for marketing, positioning, and reselling AI solutions
- Engage and grow hyperscaler relationships (GCP, Azure, AWS)
- Build meaningful, long-lasting partnerships; act as ambassador and trusted advisor
- Own joint business plans with each partner -- review cadence, engagement levels, mutual accountability
- Expand market reach, brand visibility, and customer value through the partner ecosystem

### Requirements

- 5+ years leading B2B SaaS GTM partnership organizations
- Working knowledge of negotiating, deploying, and running B2B SaaS channel (reseller) partnerships
- Track record building multi-channel GTM alongside a strong direct sales team
- Strong interpersonal, persuasion, and teamwork skills
- Some experience with hyperscalers (GCP, AWS, Azure)
- Track record navigating the AI partnership ecosystem
- Experience enhancing technical partnerships with commercial referral / co-marketing / co-selling agreements

### Compensation (from Glassdoor listing)

- Base salary range: $180K-$240K
- Equity likely significant given $4.5B valuation and stage
- Benefits: 100% dependent health/dental/vision coverage, flexible PTO, winter break shutdown
