DailyPay integrates with 180+ HCM systems — this role requires deep familiarity with that ecosystem.

2. **Years of experience (minor).** 12+ years required vs ~10+ years in partnerships/BD. Founder years fill the gap in total career experience but may not satisfy strict HR screening.

3. **Team management at VP scale.** The role manages Channel Directors — Ari has built from zero but hasn't explicitly run a layered partnerships org with Director-level reports.
