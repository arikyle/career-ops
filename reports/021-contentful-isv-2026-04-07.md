## C) Level and Strategy

**JD level:** Director
**Ari's natural level for this archetype:** Director/VP

This is a level match. The role reports to VP of Corporate Development & Strategy, which is a strong reporting line. The scope (ISV ecosystem + marketplace + small team) is consistent with Director level at a company of Contentful's size (~700 employees).

**"Sell senior without lying" plan:**
- Lead with the zero-to-one builder narrative: "I've built three partnership functions from scratch -- at i2o, Profitero, and Amobee. Each time I defined the partner archetypes, built the program infrastructure, and drove revenue from zero."
- Emphasize the strategic + execution duality the JD asks for: "I don't just set strategy -- I run the motions. At i2o I closed ~$500K in new ARR myself while simultaneously designing the partner program."
- Position founder experience as ecosystem thinking: "Running five ventures taught me to think like a platform -- where do partners add value, how do you make it easy for them to build, and how do you align incentives."

**"If downleveled" plan:**
- Unlikely at Director level, but if offered Senior Director or similar with lower comp: accept if total comp meets $300K target with equity factored in. Negotiate 6-month review with clear promotion criteria tied to the "expected outcomes" in the JD.
