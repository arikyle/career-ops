### Gaps Analysis

**1. Years of Experience (15+ required vs ~11-12 actual)**
- Severity: Moderate blocker. The gap is 3-4 years.
- Mitigation: Frame the founder years (2019-present) as concurrent BD/partnership experience. Multiple simultaneous ventures + full-time roles show density of experience beyond linear years. Cover letter angle: "My 11+ years span both enterprise partnership leadership and founding 5 ventures — the intensity and breadth of experience exceeds what linear tenure captures."

**2. People Leadership (7+ years required)**
- Severity: Hard blocker. No mention of direct reports in CV. This is a VP role leading an existing team.
- Mitigation: Difficult. Cross-functional program leadership and managing 30 agents at RealiFi are adjacent but not the same as managing a partnerships team at a $3.3B company. Could frame brokerage management (30 agents) as people leadership, but it's a stretch for this context.

**3. Employee Benefits Ecosystem**
- Severity: Moderate. Listed as "strongly preferred" not required.
- Mitigation: Frame retail media ecosystem knowledge as transferable. B2B enterprise SaaS partnership motions are similar regardless of vertical. Weight MD shows healthcare interest. Cover letter: "While my partnership experience is rooted in retail media and SaaS, the ecosystem mechanics — carrier relationships, benefit technology integrations, employer distribution — map directly to motions I've built before."

## C) Level & Strategy

**Detected level:** VP (reports to CRO)
**Ari's natural level for this archetype:** Director / borderline VP

This is a stretch role. The 15+ years requirement and 7+ years people leadership are above Ari's current profile. At Spring Health's scale ($3.3B valuation, 450+ clients), they likely want someone who has managed a partnerships org of 5-15 people at a similar-stage company.

**"Sell senior without lying" plan:**
- Lead with the zero-to-one builder angle: "I've built three partnership functions from scratch — that's rarer than managing an existing one."
- Frame i2o as VP-level scope: first partnerships hire, enterprise deals, P&L ownership, product strategy influence.
- Frame the ventures as concurrent leadership experience, not a detour.
- Acknowledge the team management gap honestly: "I'm looking to formalize the people leadership I've been doing informally across my ventures and cross-functional programs."

**"If downleveled" plan:**
- A Senior Director or Director of Partnerships role at Spring Health would be a strong fit.
- Accept if base is $200K+ with clear path to VP and 6-month review.
- This company's trajectory (Series E, $3.3B) means equity could be meaningful.
