## Company Overview

Black Forest Labs is the research lab behind Latent Diffusion and Stable Diffusion -- breakthroughs that made generative AI accessible to millions. Their FLUX models are best-in-class for image/video generation, topping public benchmarks and powering creative tools worldwide. ~50-person team based in Freiburg (Germany) and San Francisco. Frontier AI research lab with open-source roots and a growing commercial layer.

## Role Summary

Senior Partnerships Manager responsible for architecting the commercial layer of generative AI technology. This is not a quota-carrying sales role -- it's about designing pricing models, structuring enterprise and API partnerships, forging distribution channels, and translating research breakthroughs into market opportunities. Reports likely to leadership; works directly with Research, Engineering, Finance, and Legal.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Drive business growth through strategic partnerships and custom deal development
- Develop pricing strategies for new capabilities (API, enterprise, on-premise)
- Lead complex product, data, and distribution deals cross-functionally
- Identify and forge new distribution and monetization channels
- Translate technical capabilities into commercial opportunities
- Structure partnership models for both API users and on-premise enterprises

**Location:** Freiburg (Germany) or San Francisco (USA)
**Comp:** $160K–$225K base (SF)

## Archetype

**Primary:** Strategic Partnerships Leader + Platform & Ecosystem Partnerships
**Secondary:** AI/Tech Commercialization Lead
